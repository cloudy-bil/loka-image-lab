A QUICK GUIDE

Resize, convert, or compress an image online

Loka Image Lab is a free online image resizer and converter for everyday graphics. Adjust width and height, keep the original proportions, or choose a preset for a social post, story, profile picture, banner, or print page. Convert supported images to JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, TIFF, or SVG; AVIF export appears when your browser can create it.

Need a smaller image? Use the compression controls to choose an output format, image edge, and quality. For a tiled post, split a picture into rows and columns and save each tile separately—no ZIP required. The PDF tools can turn images into a PDF, export PDF pages as PNG or JPG, split pages into separate PDF files, and merge selected PDFs.

Image and PDF processing happens in your browser. SVG export wraps a PNG image in an SVG file; it does not convert a photograph into editable vector artwork. Supported imports and AVIF encoding depend on your browser.

Loka Image Lab is free to use and does not require an account. Your files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to a Loka server.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

The useful details, before you drop a file.

Is Loka Image Lab free to use?

Yes. The image and PDF tools are free to use and do not require an account.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

Image and PDF processing runs in your browser. Your selected files are not uploaded to a Loka processing server.

Which image formats can I convert?

You can export JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, TIFF, and SVG. AVIF export is available when your browser supports it. SVG export embeds a raster image; it does not trace the picture into editable vector paths.

Can I resize images for social media?

Yes. Choose custom dimensions or a preset for common social posts, stories, banners, avatars, and print sizes.

Can I convert or split PDF files?

Yes. You can turn images into a PDF, export PDF pages as PNG or JPG, split PDF pages into separate files, and merge PDFs in your browser.

Does Loka Image Lab convert images to vector graphics?

No. SVG export wraps a PNG image in an SVG file; it does not convert a photograph into editable vector artwork.
